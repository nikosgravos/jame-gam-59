# 🍅 The Fruit that Remained

An immortal tomato has appeared throughout history wherever an apple was meant to be.
Explore strange events, solve mysterious puzzles, and uncover the truth behind the fruit that humanity was never meant to remember.

## Gameplay

**The Fruit that Remained** is a short narrative puzzle game about a rewritten history hiding in plain sight. 
You'll solve puzzles, decrypt cryptograms, crack codes, and slowly reconstruct the truth.

## Development

Built in **Unity 6 (6000.6.2f1)** during the 48-hour jam window. As a two-day solo-scoped narrative puzzle project, the game is structured around a handful of self-contained systems:

- A scripted intro sequence to set up the game's premise
- A minigame, a cryptogram puzzle and a separate code-entry puzzle, each gating story progression
- A scripted sequence tying the historical vignettes together
- A final riddle sequence that closes out the story
- Background music and ambient audio support
